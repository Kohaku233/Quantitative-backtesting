## Design Context

### Users
This workspace serves two audiences:

- self-directed developers and quantitative researchers who are comfortable iterating on strategy code
- less technical traders or investors who still need to understand whether a strategy is credible

The product must support both rapid experimentation and careful result interpretation. The core job is validating whether a strategy is effective across a selected historical range, with enough clarity that users can trust the output without digging through raw implementation details.

### Brand Personality
Calm, professional, rational.

The interface should feel credible, stable, and deliberate. It should communicate analytical seriousness rather than hype. The emotional goal is confidence through clarity: users should feel that the platform is helping them inspect a strategy, not selling them a fantasy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained research-terminal aesthetic with high readability and high data visibility. Favor clean structure, disciplined spacing, clear typographic hierarchy, and visual calm over decorative flourish.

The interface must not resemble a generic admin dashboard and must not feel flashy, noisy, or cyberpunk. It should avoid ornamental gradients, glowing effects, or over-designed finance cliches. Both light and dark themes should be supported, with simple, direct theme treatment rather than dramatic stylistic shifts.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize readable analysis over decoration. Every visual decision must improve comprehension of data, workflow state, or result confidence.
2. Preserve calm density. Show enough information for serious research without collapsing into clutter or dashboard boilerplate.
3. Make structure obvious. Inputs, market context, metrics, and evidence trails should feel clearly separated and easy to scan.
4. Use restrained visual language. Strong typography, spacing, and contrast should carry the design more than effects or ornament.
5. Keep trust high. Error states, sync phases, and backtest outcomes must feel explicit, sober, and technically credible in both light and dark themes.
